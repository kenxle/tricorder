# Brasswick: two-minute pitch script

Read this out loud at a steady pace. Each time stamp marks where that part should begin.

**0:00 Who we are**

Hi, I'm Celia Brandvold, co-founder and CEO of Brasswick. Freight brokers pay thousands of carrier invoices every month. Many of them carry charges nobody agreed to, like detention, lumper fees or the same invoice sent twice. Settlement teams check a few by hand and pay the rest.

**0:20 What it does**

Brasswick reads every carrier invoice and matches it to the load. It checks the rate confirmation, the bill of lading and the proof of delivery. Clean invoices go through. The ones that do not match are held, with the reason, and we write the dispute to the carrier.

**0:40 Traction**

We started charging in May 2025. Revenue over the last twelve months was $943,340. 14 brokers pay us today, and 14 of the 15 who have ever paid us are still with us.

**1:00 How we make money**

Brokers pay a monthly subscription based on how many loads they move. On invoices they paid before they joined us, we chase the overbilled money back from carriers and keep 30 percent of what we recover.

**1:15 Team**

I ran carrier settlement at a freight brokerage for nine years. My co-founder, Marcus Ashdown, built document extraction for insurance claims software. We have 7 full-time people today.

**1:35 The ask**

We are raising three million dollars in a seed round. We will hire 7 people, mostly in sales and engineering. With the hires in our plan, that gives us close to three years of runway.

**1:50 Close**

Thank you. I'm Celia, and I would love to show you a live invoice.
