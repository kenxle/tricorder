# Hollis Voice: two-minute pitch script

Read this out loud on your phone. Keep a steady pace. Each time stamp marks where that part should begin.

**0:00 Who we are**

Hi, I'm Imogen Tarrant, co-founder and CEO of Hollis Voice. Independent veterinary clinics miss calls. The front desk is busy with people at the counter, the phone rings out, and the pet owner calls the next clinic. The clinic never learns that the appointment was lost.

**0:20 What it does**

Hollis Voice is an AI phone agent. It answers every call, day and night. It books the appointment, sends refill requests to the staff, and texts the pet owner a confirmation. It plugs into the clinic's existing phone line, so there is nothing new for the staff to learn.

**0:35 Traction**

We started charging in January 2025. Since then we have grown every quarter. Today we're at about three million dollars in annual recurring revenue.

**0:50 Clinics, retention and our largest customer**

That is 357 clinics on Hollis. Net revenue retention is a little over one hundred percent, so the clinics we win spend more over time. Our biggest customer runs Hollis Voice across seventeen locations, and it is still under nine percent of ARR. The base is spread out.

**1:10 Why we win**

The other options are answering services that read a script, or generic AI receptionists that do not know a vet clinic. We built for vet clinics. Vaccine reminders, refill requests and emergency routing work from day one. Our agent knows the difference between a booking and a poisoning.

**1:30 Team**

My co-founder, Teodoro Brandt, spent eleven years building speech systems at a telecom software company. I ran operations for a regional veterinary group. We know both the phone and the front desk.

We are raising nine million dollars. We plan to hire twenty-four people over the next year, mostly engineers and salespeople. At our plan, that gives us 37 months of runway.

Thank you. I'm Imogen, and I would love to show you a live call.
