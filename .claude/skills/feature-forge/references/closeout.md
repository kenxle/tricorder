# Close the feature record

Closeout applies to every route, including small changes. Keep it proportional:
a compact spec may hold the entire record. Use existing project locations and
authority rather than introducing another board or a required cleanup plugin.

- Finish the release record: actual landed and deployed revisions, flag state,
  acceptance evidence, independent findings and dispositions, and any unverified
  conditions. Follow [post-launch verification](post-launch.md) where applicable.
- Commit and push the authorized work. Follow through on required checks and
  review corrections. Do not include unrelated edits or claim work is published
  when it remains local.
- Update ongoing documentation, diagrams, and their indexes when behavior,
  contracts, setup, or structure changed. Link the feature's decisions instead of
  copying its history into durable docs.
- Capture surprises and useful lessons as they occur. Record what happened,
  evidence, why it mattered, and the change that prevents repetition. A lesson
  explains what was learned. A bug ticket tracks unfinished repair. Keep both
  when needed and link them.
- Update the existing board with unfinished work, owners, and dependencies.
  Close completed items and leave unresolved decisions visible. Bring the stable
  Progress page up to date with the result and next action, or an explicit None.
- Clean only owned temporary resources, branches, and worktrees as authorized.
  Check for uncommitted or unpushed work first. Preserve review sources and
  evidence still referenced by the record. Respect project deletion rules and
  list deferred cleanup rather than discarding another agent's work.

Use the [local principle additions](local-principles.md) to turn recurring lessons
into a check, type, script, or narrower instruction. Propose changes to project
instructions when their approval is required. A quiet small change may have no
new lesson or diagram change. Record that judgment rather than creating filler.
