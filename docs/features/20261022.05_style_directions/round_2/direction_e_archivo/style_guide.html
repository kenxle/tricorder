Style guide, Direction E

Black Card

The quiet wealth of a charge card, crossed with the precision of a trading terminal. Warm graphite, off-white type and one pale metal, kept to hairlines, the primary button and the band of the range strip.

Colour

Dark first. Light is white with graphite ink.

Every colour is a token. Dark is the default. The light theme swaps the ground to white and the metal to a deeper brass so it reads on paper. The homepage hero and closing band stay dark in both themes.

Twelve columns, one gutter, three corners.

GridBootstrap 5.3 for columns only. Container 1264px with 32px side padding on desktop, 16px on phones. Every heading starts on column one.

Rules

Rules a builder can apply without asking.

The metal is for edges

Use it for hairlines, the primary button, focus and the band. Never as a large fill.

A thin line of gold reads as precision. A field of it reads as decoration.

Drama lives on the homepage

Only the homepage hero and closing band stay dark in the light theme, and only the hero panel is raised.

The product screens are for work. They stay quiet and get out of the way.

Headings on column one

Every section heading starts on the container's left edge, the same line as the content under it. No labels in a side column.

A reader checks alignment by eye. One edge is easy to trust.

Cards in a row match

Side-by-side boxes share one structure and one height. Their chips or actions sit at the foot.

Uneven boxes look like an error, and an error is the last thing a partner should see.

Headlines are tight

Display at 0.94 leading, section headings at 1.02, both with negative tracking.

Loose leading on large type looks unfinished.

Figures in mono

Every number, file name and place is IBM Plex Mono with tabular figures. Stated sits beside found, at the same size.

The partner compares numbers down a column. They must line up.

Never colour alone

Each mark pairs a glyph with a word. Out of band is a triangle as well as a colour.

Some readers cannot tell red from green, and printouts lose colour.

Chips on their own row

Source chips go under the sentence they support, never inside it.

A chip in mid-sentence breaks the line the reader is following.

One radius scale

4px for marks and chips, 8px for buttons and inputs, 14px for panels. Tables and strips stay square.

Three sizes read as a system. More read as accident.

Hover keeps contrast

Hover changes the fill or the border, never the text to a fainter colour. Check both themes.

A control that fades when you point at it feels broken.

The synthetic label once

The synthetic data room label appears once per page, next to the readout excerpt.

Said once it is a fact. Said everywhere it is noise.
