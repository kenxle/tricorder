Style guide, Direction F

Instrument

Tricorder should look like an expensive instrument: a white working surface, a strict 12-column grid, and every number drawn against its range. The homepage gets one deep ground for drama; the product stays quiet and gets out of the way.

01 Direction

Round one's Reference Range, rebuilt to a stricter standard.

Kept

The range strip as the signature. The ARR disagreement as a number line over a source table. Stated beside found. The Ask the founder prompt. A white ground and Archivo at 87.5% width for display.

Raised

A strict 12-column grid with one gutter. A larger, tighter type scale. One radius scale: 6px on controls, 12px on panels. No shadows on working screens. One accent, used for action only.

Added

A deep blue-black ground for the homepage hero and closing band, and a large product surface that straddles the hero and the page. New body and data faces: Instrument Sans and IBM Plex Mono.

How it is built

Two cells of one structure: a label, the figure in Archivo at 40px, and the source in Plex Mono under it. The found figure sits on the right, the way a reader scans from claim to check. The difference sits on its own line with exact dollars.

The prompt under it is the Ask the founder component: one question, written so the operator can paste it into an email, and one button that adds it to the ask list. It is the only place outside a button or link where the accent fills a box.

UseNever colour the stated figure red. The mark says which piece is weak; the two figures stay ink so neither reads as the right answer.

12 Rules

Rules a builder can apply without asking.

01
Put everything on the 12-column grid with one gutter: 32px, or 16px below 992px.
Readers check alignment by eye. One shared left edge is what makes a page read as ordered.
02
Start every section heading on the same column as the content under it.
A label floating in its own narrow column breaks the left edge and reads as decoration.
03
Boxes in one row share one structure and one height.
Uneven bottoms look like an accident, and the eye compares boxes by their edges.
04
Use two radii for boxes: 6px on controls, chips and marks; 12px on panels and control groups. Tables and rules stay square. The band inside a strip gets a 3px softening, nothing more.
One scale reads as one hand. A rounded table stops looking like a table.
05
No shadows on working screens. The homepage's product surface is the one exception.
Depth on a dense screen adds noise. The hero surface is the one object meant to look physical.
06
Use the accent for action and reference only: links, buttons, focus, the band, the ask prompt.
Blue must never read as a verdict, so the four verdict colours keep their meaning.
07
Every verdict mark carries a shape, a word and a colour.
The memo gets printed, and some partners do not see red and green apart.
08
One shape, one meaning: square strong, triangle weak, half square mixed, dashed square not enough, ring running.
The triangle is the out-of-range mark on the strip. Reusing a shape elsewhere dilutes it.
09
Dashed means absent: gaps and not enough in the room, nothing else.
Absence should look different from a finding.
10
Colour a flag on its mark only. No coloured stripe down one edge of a box, no tinted panel.
A page of red panels reads as alarm. The readout says a flag once, at the size it is.
11
Draw a band only where sources agree within 1%, or where a check rebuilt the figure.
A disagreement gets no band, so the strip never picks a winner.
12
A table is one surface. The head row keeps the body's fill and gets a stronger rule under it.
A filled head row splits one object into two and makes the table look like a template.
13
Figures in tables, strips and chips are Plex Mono, right aligned in tables. Figures in sentences stay in Instrument Sans with tabular figures.
Columns of dollars have to line up to be compared. A sentence should read as one voice.
14
Every figure has a source chip, on its own row under the sentence.
Every claim links to its source. A chip inside a sentence breaks the line it supports.
15
Set display type in Archivo at 87.5% width, 24px and up, with leading from 0.9 to 1.12.
Loose leading on a large headline looks amateur. Condensed letters crowd below 24px.
16
Keep the deep ground for the homepage: the hero, the product surface and the closing band.
Drama belongs on the page that sells. The product and the memo stay quiet so the numbers lead.
17
On hover, darken the accent in light and lighten it in dark. Text keeps its colour.
A hover that drops contrast hides the control at the moment someone reaches for it.
18
Show the synthetic label once per page, near the readout.
Repeated, it becomes wallpaper. Once, next to the figures, it is read.
19
No motion beyond a short hover fade, and none under reduced motion.
Tricorder states. It does not alarm.
