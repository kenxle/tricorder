Style directions, round two

Pick one, or name what to take from each. Round one (A, B, C) was rejected as not professional enough; these three were built to the round-two brief. Each card links the full home page and style guide. Comment anywhere on this page.

Direction D: Private Bank

The quiet confidence of a private bank or a top law firm. White working surfaces, one deep navy band (#0b1a2e), and one accent: a deep spruce green (#1d5a47; #7cc4a4 in dark) for action only.

Direction E: Black Card

the quiet wealth of a premium charge card, crossed with the precision of a trading terminal. Warm graphite ground (#131210), off-white ink (#eeebe4), and one pale champagne metal kept to hairlines, the primary button, focus and the range strip's band.

Direction F: Instrument

keep what Ken liked in A (the range strip, the ARR number line over its source table, stated beside found, the Ask the founder prompt, a white ground, Archivo at 87.5% width). Then put it all on a strict 12-column grid (1312px container, 32px gutter, 16px below 992px), with a larger and tighter type scale, two radii (6px controls, 12px panels) and no shadows on working screens.
