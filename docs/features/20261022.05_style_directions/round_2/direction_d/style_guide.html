Style guide, Direction D

Private Bank

The quiet confidence of a private bank or a top law firm: white working surfaces, one deep navy band, a sober serif for headlines and a plain sans for everything read. The homepage gets one dramatic moment, the lifted readout card on navy. The product gets out of the way.

Colour

White to work on, navy for the one deep band.

Type

A sober serif to head, a plain sans to read, a mono for figures.

Source Serif 4 is set at its display optical size, tight and medium weight, for headlines only. IBM Plex Sans carries every sentence and control. IBM Plex Mono carries figures and file names, always tabular.

Space, corners, lines

Twelve columns, one gutter, two radii.

The page is a 12-column grid with a 24px gutter, 1248px of content at most, with 32px sides on desktop and 16px on a phone. Space steps by 8.

Rules

Rules a builder can apply without asking.

RuleWhy
Working surfaces are white. Navy is for the homepage hero, the closing band and a page header, nothing else.One deep band reads as gravitas. A second colour of ground turns it into a theme, and grey grounds read as dull.
Only the readout card on the hero is lifted with a shadow. Every other panel is a 1px outline.One lifted surface tells the eye where the product is. Shadows on every card flatten that.
Two radii: 6px for chips, buttons, inputs, marks and the ask box; 12px for panels. Chart marks stay square.A small, fixed scale looks deliberate. A rounded tick blurs the position it marks.
Every element sits in a column of the 12-column grid with the 24px gutter. A heading starts on the same line as the content under it.The page should line up by eye. A label floating in a side column breaks the edge.
Boxes in one row share one structure and one height. Use equal-height columns, or a subgrid when rows inside must line up too.Ragged bottoms read as unfinished.
The accent marks action only: buttons, links, focus, the ask box, the steered note. It never marks a verdict.Green flag green is its own token. Keeping the two apart stops a button reading as a judgment.
Every verdict mark carries a shape, a word and a colour: filled circle strong, triangle weak, half circle mixed, open circle not enough.Memos get printed and read by colour-blind partners.
Colour a flag on its mark and its stated figure only. Never on a panel fill or a single edge of a box.A page of red panels reads as alarm. The readout says a flag once, at the size it is.
Figures and file names are Plex Mono with tabular figures. A figure inside a sentence stays in Plex Sans, with tabular figures on.Columns of dollars line up to be compared. A sentence reads as one voice.
Source chips go on their own row under the sentence they support.A chip in mid-line breaks the sentence and the line height.
A table is one surface: rules between rows, the head row the same fill as the body.A filled head row splits one table into two boxes.
Serif headlines keep leading between 1.02 and 1.15, tracked slightly tight.Loose leading on a large serif looks amateur.
Hover darkens a control on white and lightens it on navy or in dark. Check every button in both themes.A hover that loses contrast hides the label at the moment of the click.
The synthetic label appears once per page, beside the first readout excerpt.Once is honest. Repeated, it becomes wallpaper.
No textures, no rotated elements, no coloured bars above headings.The surface stays quiet so the figures carry the page.
