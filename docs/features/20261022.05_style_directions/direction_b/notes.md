# Direction B: Manifest

- **Name:** Manifest. Every readout line is a manifest line: what the company stated, set beside what Tricorder found, with the stamp it earned.
- **From the motif, structure:** numbered lines (L.01 for claims, P.01 for pieces) in a ruled gutter, and box labels on every panel ("Box 4, Takes"), so a partner can say "line 2" in the meeting.
- **From the motif, the declared and inspected columns:** each flag shows Stated on the left and Found on the right, and only the found side gets the 3px amber bar. The on-screen words stay the readout's own (stated, found, corroborated), so the customs vocabulary never enters the copy.
- **From the motif, stamps and seals:** every mark is an inked stamp (word, shape and colour, double border, light grit, tilted 1.5 degrees only at a line head), and the source chip carries a small diamond seal. A double rule closes a slip, like the total on a form. Dashed lines mean only one thing: absent or not real (gaps, not enough in the room, the synthetic label).
- **Colour:** dark-first on petrol (hue 197), so the hue-27 amber sits on its complement and reads as a signal lamp. Amber marks only the next action and the found value, never a verdict. Weak is rose (hue 350) to keep it clear of the amber.
- **Risk taken:** Barlow Semi Condensed, a DIN-style ledger face (it replaced Libre Caslon Display after review: too bookish), on a dark ground at very large sizes, with whole screens given to one sentence. It reads calm and expensive at desktop size. It could feel slow to an associate who wants density, and the readout itself would need a denser mode than this homepage shows.
- **Unsure of:** whether stamps read as serious or as costume once a whole overview of nine pieces is stamped; the grit mask is light, and it could be dropped. The style guide's strong, mixed and not-enough verdict lines keep [brackets] because BRAND.md gives no Hollis Voice figure for those marks. The light theme is a faithful mirror, but it is less distinctive than the dark one.
