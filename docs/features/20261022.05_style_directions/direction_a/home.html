01 / The readout

Each number sits against its range.

Where the files agree, the number sits inside the band. Where a check rebuilds it and the files fall outside, the readout marks it out of range and shows both numbers.

02 / Who it is for

Two people at each fund, two promises.

Tricorder is for seed and Series A venture firms. The associate works in the readout. The partner reads the memo it helps write.

The associate, who prepares the memo

The gathering is done before you look.

"Before I even open your data room, I already have ten tabs up." (associate at a seed-focused fund, Jan 2026)

The tabs, the cross-checks and the gaps are done when the deck arrives, and every claim in the memo links to its file.

  • Start at the best green flags and the worst red flags.
  • Open a weak piece to see the files that disagree, side by side.
  • Steer any piece in one sentence. The checks' numbers stay on screen beside the change.
The partner, who reads the memo and decides

A memo you can check yourself.

"When burn or revenue differs between the deck, the model and the accounting records, the gap suggests carelessness or something worse, and either reading damages the deal." (seed fund guide, Aug 2026)

Any line opens its sources in two clicks, and you can change the model's assumptions to see what the deal needs to be true.

  • Each piece of the company is marked looking strong or looking weak, with its reason in one line.
  • Your what-ifs sit beside the company's figure and the rebuilt one, marked as yours.
  • The call stays yours. The readout never makes it.
03 / What Tricorder adds

Other tools turn a room into a memo. Here is what this one adds.

Several tools already link a memo to its sources, and one fund partner built his own. That shows funds want it.

01The best green flags and the worst red flags first, then an overview of every piece of the company, strong and weak.
02Sources shown both ways: the files that agree on a number as well as the ones that disagree.
03Numbers checked by scripts, not by a model's reading, and verdicts set by a stated rule.
04Contract terms read against how each contract is counted.
05Gaps written as gaps, and an ask list for the founder.
06An interactive memo each partner can steer and run what-ifs on.
07Checks beyond the room: the pitch video, comps and the associate's usual tabs.
04 / What it is not

Outside the range.

NotA sourcing database or a CRM.
NotConfirmatory diligence.
NotA replacement for the partner's judgment.

Tricorder reads only the documents you give it or the decks in mail you send it, keeps only the readout and its sources, and lets you see and delete everything it pulled.
