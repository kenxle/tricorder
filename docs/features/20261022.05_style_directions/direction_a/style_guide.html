01The direction

Every number sits against its range, the way a vital sign sits against its normal band. A red flag is the out-of-range mark: a small red triangle on the stated figure, with the rebuilt figure inside the band beside it.

It reads like a lab report and a bedside monitor, not like a hospital. That means hairlines, square corners, tabular figures, a reference column, and one mark for out of range. There are no crosses, no heartbeat lines and no scrubs green.

06The range strip

found 19 mo stated 37 mo
02040 months
  1. Track: the scale, with a light tick every tenth.
  2. Band: where sources fall within 1% of each other, or the figure a check rebuilt. Accent tint.
  3. Tick: one source's figure. A spoken figure ("about") is a hatched, wider tick.
  4. Out-of-range mark: the red triangle over a stated figure the check did not rebuild.
  5. Axis: the scale's ends and middle, in mono.
UseUnder a green flag, a red flag or a disagreement, above its table. A disagreement draws no band, because the readout does not pick a winner. Labels may round ($2.24M); the table under the strip carries the exact figure.

12Rules a builder can apply without asking

  1. The accent marks action and reference only: links, buttons, focus, chip edges and the band.Blue must never read as a verdict.
  2. Every verdict mark carries a shape, a word and a colour. Never colour alone.The memo is printed and read by colour-blind partners.
  3. One shape, one meaning: filled square strong, triangle weak, half square mixed, dashed square not enough, open ring running.The triangle is the monitor's out-of-range mark. Reusing it elsewhere dilutes it.
  4. Dashed means absent. Use it for gaps, not-enough, and the synthetic label, nothing else.Absence should look different from a finding.
  5. Corners are 0. Panels separate with a 1px hairline. No shadows.At this density, shadows and rounding add noise without adding order.
  6. Colour a flag on its 3px left edge and its mark, never on the whole panel.A page of red panels reads as alarm. The readout says a flag once, at the size it is.
  7. Figures in tables, strips and chips are Fragment Mono, right aligned in tables. A figure inside a sentence stays in Atkinson, with tabular figures on.Columns of dollars have to line up to be compared, and a sentence should read as one voice. Atkinson's slashed zero still keeps 0 apart from O.
  8. Every figure has a source chip beside it or in its row. A figure without one does not ship.Every claim links to its source. That is a product commitment.
  9. Draw a band only where sources agree within 1%, or where a check rebuilt the figure. A disagreement gets no band.The readout lists sources side by side and does not pick a winner.
  10. Green flags and red flags share one size, weight and length.A strength gets the same care as a problem.
  11. Bricolage only at 20px and up. Anything read in sentences is Atkinson.Bricolage's character breaks down when small. Atkinson is built for legibility.
  12. The chart-paper grid sits behind the hero and the call to action only, never behind data.Grid lines compete with the ticks on a strip.
  13. No motion except the running ring, and none at all under reduced motion.A blinking monitor is an alarm. Tricorder states, it does not alarm.
