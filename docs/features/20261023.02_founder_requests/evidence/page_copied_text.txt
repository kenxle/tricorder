Subject: Brasswick: documents for our review

Hi Celia,

Thank you for the Brasswick data room. To finish our first pass, could you send the items below? A short note with each file is plenty. If something does not exist yet, just say so; that helps us too.

1. Hiring plan
Please send the hiring plan for the next 12 to 18 months: role, department, start date, base pay and equity grant for each planned hire.
   What it is: A list of the people you plan to add, one row per hire.
   Best form: A CSV or a spreadsheet tab with one row per planned hire: role, department, start date, base pay and equity grant.
   A good one shows: The next 12 to 18 months; pay and start date for every hire; start dates tied to milestones.

2. Product roadmap and demo
Please send a recorded demo or a login to a sandbox, and a one-page roadmap with dates and owners. Say which parts of the demo are live.
   What it is: A short plan of what the team will build and when, and a way to see the product work.
   Best form: A one-page roadmap with dates and owners, and a recorded demo or a sandbox login.
   A good one shows: Which parts of the demo are live; milestones tied to the money in this round.

3. Incorporation documents and bylaws
Please send the filed certificate of incorporation with every amendment, the current bylaws, the organizational board consent, the founders' stock purchase agreements and 83(b) elections, and a good standing certificate dated within the last few months.
   What it is: The company's formation set: the filed certificate of incorporation with amendments, the bylaws and the first board consent.
   Best form: File-stamped PDFs, plus a good standing certificate dated in the last few months.
   A good one shows: The full set, with every amendment; founder stock agreements with vesting and 83(b) elections.

4. IP and invention assignment agreements
Please send the signed invention assignment agreements for each founder (covering any work before incorporation), each employee and each contractor who has written code, designs or content, and a list of anyone who has not signed.
   What it is: The signed agreements by which founders, employees and contractors give the company what they create for it.
   Best form: Signed PDFs, one per person, plus a list of anyone who has not signed.
   A good one shows: A signed agreement from every founder, employee and contractor who has built product; founder agreements covering work from before the company; signing dates on or before each start date.

5. Board and stockholder minutes and consents
Please send the board and stockholder consents or minutes that approved each SAFE, note, stock issuance and option grant, the equity plan, and the 409A report.
   What it is: The written record of decisions by the board and the stockholders: minutes of meetings and written consents.
   Best form: Signed PDFs, in date order, as they sit in your minute book.
   A good one shows: A consent for every stock issuance, SAFE and option grant; approvals that match the cap table line for line.

Thank you.
