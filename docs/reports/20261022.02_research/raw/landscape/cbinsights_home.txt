Set the strategy
See the entire landscape before you zoom in. Identify the most promising markets based on analyst opinion, funding trends, and talent shifts. Anticipate competitor moves with strategy maps that analyze their activity.
Screen for prospects
Filter 13 million companies across dozens of attributes. Get recommendations for similar companies based on desired degrees of product and customer overlap. Track top founders and technical talent to detect startups before they launch.
Test for fit
Confirm strategic fit across technology, market, and business model. Evaluate traction using customers, partners, hiring, patents, news, and more, with sources cited. Get the inside scoop from the founders and operators driving change.
Follow the money
Trace capital flows across 1.5 million deals, each one with its origins cited. Set pricing using revenue multiples and cap tables. CB Insights is the leader in recent deal coverage, with 20% of transactions reported directly from investors.
Select the winners
Challenge your thesis against our analyst ratings. Vet the leadership team using signals of past performance. Spot inflection points using Mosaic, our proprietary health score backed by the National Science Foundation.
Time your outreach
Track signals that reveal shifts in company trajectory. Engage companies based on projections of their likely funding window. Generate scouting reports and other instant insights to prep for your next meeting.
