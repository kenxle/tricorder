What you need to know about our Venture Capital Investment Memos
What data sources can V7 Go analyze for investment memos?
V7 Go analyzes pitch decks, financial models, market research reports, due diligence materials, team bios, competitive analysis, and customer references to create comprehensive investment memos.
+
How does V7 Go ensure memo consistency across deals?
V7 Go follows your firm's investment framework and evaluation criteria, ensuring consistent structure, analysis depth, and recommendation format across all investment memos.
+
Can V7 Go customize memos for different investment stages?
Yes, V7 Go adapts memo structure and focus areas based on investment stage, from seed-stage opportunity assessment to growth-stage financial analysis and market validation.
+
How does V7 Go handle confidential startup information?
V7 Go maintains enterprise-grade security with SOC 2 Type II certification, ensuring confidential startup data and investment strategies remain protected throughout the analysis process.
+
Can V7 Go integrate with existing deal management platforms?
Yes, V7 Go integrates with popular VC platforms including Carta, Affinity, and P&L systems, enabling seamless data flow and memo distribution within your existing workflow.
+
How quickly can V7 Go generate an investment memo?
V7 Go can generate comprehensive investment memos in 2-4 hours, compared to days of manual analysis, enabling faster deal evaluation and decision-making.
