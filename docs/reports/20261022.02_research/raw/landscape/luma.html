About Event

​📋 Event Description

​New York is getting its first VC hackathon, and you're building for the people who write the checks.

​Deal Flow is a one-day, in-person hackathon hosted by localhost:nyc and VCLab. You get 9 hours to build working products for the real problems VCs face every day. Partners from NYC's top funds judge live, and the best teams leave with intros to those funds.

​Problem Statement

​Venture capital runs on spreadsheets, gut feel and endless inbox triage. Firms move billions of dollars with tools that haven't changed in a decade. Your challenge is to build software that helps VCs find better companies, make faster decisions and support their founders after the check clears.

​The Challenge Theme

  • ​Sourcing: finding breakout founders before everyone else does

  • ​Diligence: market maps, competitive analysis and memo drafting

  • ​Portfolio ops: helping funds support their companies at scale

  • ​LP reporting: making fund updates less painful and more useful

​What to Expect

  • ​9 hours of focused building, 9 AM to 6 PM

  • ​Real VC problems, with investors in the room to test your ideas against

  • ​Teams of 4

  • ​Time to meet founders, engineers and investors from across NYC

  • ​Live demos to a panel of VC judges at the end of the day

  • ​Food and drinks all day

​Schedule

  • ​9:00 AM - 10:00 AM: Breakfast, Mixer and Team Formation

  • ​10:00 AM - 10:20 AM: Intros and Demos

  • ​10:30 AM - 4:30 PM: Hacking + Lunch

  • ​4:30 PM - 5:30 PM: Judging

  • ​5:30 PM - 6:00 PM: Demos and Winner Announcement

​Prizes

  • ​500 USD cash prize for the winning team.

  • ​150 USD cash prize for the team with the best content put out on social media during the hackathon.

  • ​D. J. Patel - VC | 3x exited founder.

  • ​Radhika Khandelwal builds AI agents for a living as a product leader at Wand AI, and she's spent years in the VC world seeing what makes startups stick. She'll want to see both slick code and a real business.

  • ​Bettina Sosa is co-founder and CEO of Yaven, building a local-first AI workforce for independent businesses.

  • ​Arjun Padmajan is CEO of Roundtable, where AI agents connect and work together.

  • ​Erzsébet (Elisabeth) Ábrám - Community Architect at the residency.

  • ​Akhil A. is a Staff Security engineer at Collectors, securing companies through their AI adoption, M&A and IPO Compliance journey.

​Who Should Attend

  • ​Pre-seed and seed founders

  • ​Engineers

  • ​Builders who have felt the pain of fundraising firsthand

  • ​Anyone curious about how venture capital actually works from the inside

​You don't need VC experience or a finished idea. Just show up ready to build.

​Attendance if first-come first-serve. Once we reach capacity, we won't be able to let you in.
