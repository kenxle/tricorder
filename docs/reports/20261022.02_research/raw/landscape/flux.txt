Frequently asked questions 
 What kinds of boards can I design in Flux? 
Flux supports professional multi-layer PCBs up to eight layers used in IoT devices, wearables, and robotics. Built to go all the way from prototype to production. Featured projects 
 Can AI design a PCB? 
Yes. PCB design using AI automates heavy lifting. Flux delivers AI assisted PCB design by drafting schematics, researching parts, and routing traces. This AI based PCB design process lets you edit everything in your browser. 
 Can I import projects from Altium, Cadence, or KiCad? 
Yes. Flux imports complete projects from Altium, Cadence OrCAD and Allegro, KiCad, Siemens PADS and Xpedition, Zuken CADSTAR, Eagle, EasyEDA, and DipTrace (XML export). Schematics, parts, connections, board outlines, and placement come across. Labcenter Proteus netlist and board exports bring in parts, connections, board outlines, placement, and routing. Altium, OrCAD, PADS, KiCad, Eagle, EasyEDA, and DipTrace part libraries, Allegro footprints, and JEP30 part models become Flux parts.

 Can I export for manufacturing? 
Yes. Flux exports Gerbers, drill files, BoM, pick-and-place, and standard netlists for fabrication and downstream tools. Exporting projects 
 Is my data secure? 
Yes. Flux encrypts data in transit and at rest and runs on SOC 2-aligned cloud infrastructure with SSO and project-level permissions. Read more about privacy statement. 
 How capable is the AI — and what are its limits? 
Flux provides AI for PCB design that works alongside you. As a collaborative AI for electronics, it remains transparent: explaining changes, showing its reasoning, and checking in with you before key steps. 
 How does Flux perform in the browser? 
Flux is built to feel like a desktop-class tool without installs: large, multi-layer designs run smoothly in a modern browser, with real-time collaboration built in. Learn how to setup your browser to ensure the best possible experience. 
 How much does Flux cost? 
Eligible individual plans include a free trial; trial length and prices depend on your current offer. New Teams subscriptions start paid. See the pricing page for current terms. Enterprise pricing is available on request. Plans and pricing 
 How do I get help if I’m stuck? 
You’re not alone. Our team and community are available at help@flux.ai, with docs, Youtube videos, and fast support when you need it. 
 How is Flux different from traditional ECAD tools? 
Flux is full ECAD rebuilt for the AI era: browser-based, collaborative, connected to live parts data, and guided by explainable AI. See KiCAD vs. Flux, EasyEDA vs. Flux to learn about key differences.
