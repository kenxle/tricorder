AI-Powered Due Diligence

Everything investment teams need to evaluate deals faster

01

Automated data room analysis

Upload financial statements, cap tables, legal agreements, pitch decks, compliance documents, and board materials. askRIA reads and analyses every document automatically.

02

Diligence question extraction

Generate investment-specific diligence questions directly from the data room, including financial, legal, operational, governance, and market-related risks.

03

AI risk detection

Identify red flags such as cap table issues, unusual liquidation preferences, legal gaps, inconsistent financial projections, related-party transactions, governance concerns, and key-person dependency.

04

Evidence-based diligence scoring

Every diligence question is scored against the evidence contained within the data room, providing a consistent and transparent due diligence framework.

05

Investment readiness scoring

Generate a standardised DD Score across financial health, legal readiness, governance, team quality, market traction, and investment risk factors.

06

Custom diligence frameworks

Apply fund-specific investment criteria, sector-specific diligence requirements, and stage-specific evaluation frameworks automatically.

07

Investment committee memo generation

Generate draft IC memos directly from diligence findings, financial analysis, risk factors, and investment thesis inputs.

08

Founder request management

Convert missing information, unanswered questions, and incomplete diligence items into structured founder requests ready for review and approval.

09

Audit trail & compliance

Maintain version history, review logs, scoring records, and memo revisions to support governance, LP reporting, and compliance requirements.
