01 — Routing modes

Name the job, not the model.

Five routing slugs pick the model per request. Pin a specific model whenever you need to, or hand the gateway an ordered fallback list.

  • nicrron/auto sends long prompts to a heavy model and short ones to a fast one.
  • Fallback arrays cascade on 429 or 5xx before the first streamed byte.
  • Every request is one OpenAI-compatible call; nothing new to learn.
Read the routing guide
nicrron/auto

Picks by prompt length: over 2,000 characters goes to Claude Opus 5.5, Claude Sonnet 5.5, GPT-6 Sol or DeepSeek V4 Pro; shorter prompts to Claude Haiku 5.5, GPT-6 Luna or DeepSeek Flash.

nicrron/reasoning

DeepSeek V4 Pro, GPT-6 Sol and Claude Opus 5.5, then Kimi K3 and GLM 5.3, for multi-step logic.

nicrron/coding

Claude Opus 5.5, Claude Sonnet 5.5 and Kimi K2.7 Code, then GLM 5.3, Codestral and GPT-6 Sol.

nicrron/fastest

Claude Haiku 5.5, GPT-6 Luna and DeepSeek Flash for the lowest latency.

nicrron/cheapest

Every chat model, cheapest list price first, so the first one that answers is the cheapest available.

02 — Failover and caching

Uptime you don't have to engineer.

Provider health is scored continuously with an EWMA latency model and circuit breakers. Deterministic requests are served straight from Redis, in milliseconds, for free.

  • If a provider rate-limits or fails, the request moves to the next one instantly.
  • Exact-match cache: 12 ms and $0.00 on a hit, scoped to your account, keyed by the full request.
  • Send temperature 0 or x-nicrron-cache: true to opt in per request.

03 — Retention

We keep the bill, not your prompts.

We never store chat prompts. Completions are cached for up to 24 hours, only for your account, when temperature is 0 or you opt in.

  • Stored: latency, status, token counts and a hash of the request.
  • Never stored: chat prompts, embedding inputs or vectors, audio.
  • Send x-nicrron-cache: false and a completion is never cached.
  • Generated video and images stay 30 days for download, then are deleted.

Questions

Frequently asked.

What does a request cost?+

Each model's list price, as shown in the catalog. Requests paid from credits add a 5% platform fee. With your own provider key, the provider bills you and we charge 5% of the list price. There is no subscription.

How does the deposit match work?+

We match deposits 1:1 in bonus credits, in $100 steps, up to $500 in bonus credits per rolling 90-day period. Each match is applied after a short account review, usually within 24 hours of the deposit. Bonus credits have no cash value; the terms have the details.

Do you store my prompts?+

We never store chat prompts. Completions are cached for up to 24 hours, only for your account, when temperature is 0 or you opt in. Send x-nicrron-cache: false to turn the cache off for a request. We keep latency, status, token counts and a hash of each request for billing. Embedding inputs and audio are never stored. Video and image jobs keep their prompt and output for 30 days so you can download the result, then both are deleted.

How do the routing slugs work?+

Send one of five slugs as the model: nicrron/auto picks by prompt length, nicrron/reasoning and nicrron/coding try a fixed list of strong models in order, nicrron/fastest prefers low-latency models, and nicrron/cheapest tries the cheapest chat models first. If a provider fails or rate-limits before answering, the next one is tried.

Can I generate video, images and audio?+

Yes. POST /v1/videos/generations runs Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash, Seedance 2.5, 2.0 and 1.0 Pro, Kling O3, 3.0 and 2.5, Wan 3.0, Grok Imagine 1.5, PixVerse V6, MiniMax H3 Max, FLUX 3 Max and Hailuo 02 as async jobs, charged upfront and refunded if they fail. /v1/images/generations serves Nano Banana, GPT Image 2.5, FLUX 3, Seedream 5, Qwen Image 3, Ideogram 4.5 and Recraft V4.1. /v1/audio/speech and /v1/audio/transcriptions give OpenAI TTS and Whisper through the standard OpenAI SDK.

Do provider prompt-cache discounts reach me?+

Yes. When a provider reports a prompt-cache read, you pay its cached rate: Anthropic cache reads are 90% off and OpenAI cache reads 50% off. The cached token counts appear in the response's usage.
