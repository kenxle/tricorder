​Why Attend
​Learn and Build: This is not a code and leave event, you will be invited to our Discord community and will have office hours and mentoring sessions from the teams at LandingAI and AWS.
​Build and Demo: Present a working solution and receive feedback from industry judges and platform experts.
​Partner Access: Connect with LandingAI and AWS for pilots and collaboration opportunities.
​Win and Grow: Compete for prizes, gain visibility, and explore opportunities in the LandingAI builder program.
​Judging Criteria
​Problem clarity and domain relevance
​Depth of ADE integration and technical implementation
​Accuracy, reliability, and performance on representative documents
​Usability and workflow design for target users
​Real-world feasibility and path to pilot within 90 days
​Quality and clarity of the 4-minute presentation/demo
