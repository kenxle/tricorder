Frequently asked questions

Is this really per month?

Prices are shown per seat per month so they are easy to compare, but plans are billed annually. You pay for 12 months up front and can pay by card, bank transfer or invoice.

Can I add seats later?

Yes. Add seats at any time and pay the prorated difference. Every seat on your account moves to the lower rate as your team grows, so adding seats never costs more per seat than you are paying today.

What's included in the free trial?

A 3-day free trial with full Premium access, 50 export credits and every company profile and funding round. No credit card required.

Can I connect Dealroom to my AI assistant?

Yes. Premium Plus and Enterprise include our hosted MCP server, so Claude, Cursor, VS Code and Codex can query company, investor and funding data in plain language. It is read-only and returns only the data your licence permits. See the setup guide.

How is the API priced?

The API is an add-on to any plan, priced on the volume of calls you need rather than on seats. Tick the API add-on when you request your quote and we will price it alongside your seats.

Do you offer discounts for startups or nonprofits?

Yes. Qualified startups and nonprofit organisations get special pricing. Contact sales to check eligibility.
