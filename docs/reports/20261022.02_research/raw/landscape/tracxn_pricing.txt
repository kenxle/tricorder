Frequently asked questions
What is Tracxn Lite?
Lite gives you a chance to explore our curated data at no cost and comes with strict usage limits.
What is Export?
The Export feature allows you to download platform data for offline analysis. Exports require credits, and you can purchase additional credits upon request.
What are the Data Solutions packs?
Our Data Solutions packs enable seamless integration with your existing workflows through APIs and Datadump features. These are also available as add-ons along with your premium subscription.
What is your password sharing policy?
For security reasons, simultaneous logins from multiple devices using the same account are prohibited. Our system flags such activities. To allow multiple users access, we recommend purchasing a separate subscription for each individual.
What is enterprise grade support?
Our platform includes the MyAnalyst Tool, which allows you to submit your research queries to our team of analysts. They assist you in finding and organizing the information you need. Each user receives 1 free analyst query per month. Additional queries are billed separately on a case-by-case basis.
