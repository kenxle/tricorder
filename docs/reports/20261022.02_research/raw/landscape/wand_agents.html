01 / Identity

A worker that persists.

A continuing entity holds its mandate, context and responsibilities across interactions. The models it uses can change while its place in the organization remains.

02 / Initiative

Objectives create action.

A deadline, material signal or unresolved obligation can create the next task. The worker connects what changed with the outcome it is responsible for pursuing.

03 / Judgment

Agency within authority.

The worker reasons through a situation and acts within its access, budget and policies. Decisions outside that boundary reach the designated human or agent authority.

04 / Collaboration

Work crosses disciplines.

Agents coordinate with colleagues, request missing expertise and carry work between responsibilities. The organization can bring the right capabilities to a shared outcome.

05 / Development

Experience changes the method.

Operating episodes can reveal patterns, inspire new strategies and develop reusable skills. Validated learning becomes useful when it reaches the next relevant decision.

06 / Accountability

Results remain visible.

The worker’s actions, permissions and observed outcomes give management a basis for assessment. Performance means work completed and results verified.
