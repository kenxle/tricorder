About Harmonic
Harmonic Overview
Harmonic created the only complete startup database, enabling the right investments to reach the right entrepreneurs at the perfect moment.
Harmonic's 3 Products
Harmonic Platform: API Access
Programmatic access to Harmonic's startup intelligence database via REST and GraphQL APIs. Sold as 'API Calls' at a flat annual fee for unlimited API calls. Designed for syncing Harmonic data into CRMs (Salesforce, HubSpot, Affinity) and internal tools.
Harmonic Platform: Bulk Data
Full dataset delivery of Harmonic's complete startup intelligence database directly to a customer's data warehouse or cloud storage. Includes 35M+ company records and 195M+ professional profiles with weekly data refreshes. Delivery targets include Amazon S3, Google BigQuery, and Snowflake.
Harmonic Platform: Enterprise Console
Web-based GUI platform providing full access to Harmonic's startup intelligence database. Includes company and people search across 35M+ companies and 195M+ professional profiles, AI-powered Scout search agent, network mapping, watchlists/saved searches, CSV export, AI diligence reports, Chrome Extension, and enterprise security features.
