Why don’t you publish prices?
Because a mid-market PE fund processing 40 data rooms a year and a top-10 carrier processing two million claims are not the same buyer. We scope your workflows, volumes and user roles in one 30-minute call and return a fixed annual number within 48 hours. Most firms find it lands below the cost of a single analyst.
How quickly can we see a return?
Typically in the first quarter. Firms measure it in analyst hours returned, faster cycle times on deals or submissions, and errors caught before committee. In the pilot we agree the metric with you up front and report against it.
Can we start with one workflow and expand?
Yes. Most teams start with one high-volume workflow, such as CIM screening, submission intake or lease abstraction, prove the return, then extend to adjacent teams. Your agreement expands with document volume and users, not with a new contract each time.
What is included in the platform fee?
V7 Go, the Context Graph, all specialized agents, integrations with your data rooms, policy systems and data warehouse, role-based access, audit logs and a dedicated solutions team for deployment. There are no feature tiers.
How is our confidential deal and client data protected?
Your data is never used to train models. V7 is SOC 2 Type II and GDPR compliant, offers private deployment, and every output is cited to its source page so compliance and audit can verify any answer in one click.
