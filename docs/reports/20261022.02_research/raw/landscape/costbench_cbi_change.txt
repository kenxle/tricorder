CB Insights Pricing Overview 
CB Insights uses custom pricing — contact their sales team for a quote. The CB Insights Browser Analyst plan requires contacting sales for a custom quote and is designed for individual analysts who need quick, ai-assisted market intelligence from the browser. The CB Insights Strategy Terminal plan requires contacting sales for a custom quote and is designed for corporate strategy teams running ongoing competitive intelligence and market analysis. The CB Insights Data Solutions plan requires contacting sales for a custom quote and is designed for data teams and enterprises needing programmatic access to cb insights data for models or client-facing products. 
The median CB Insights customer pays $500/year based on 22 verified purchases. 
There are at least 7 documented hidden costs beyond CB Insights's list price, including implementation, training, and add-on fees. 
This pricing was last verified in June 9, 2026 from 1 independent source. 
Request CB Insights Pricing 
CB Insights does not publish public pricing. All three products — Browser Analyst, Strategy Terminal, and Data Solutions — are sold through direct sales with quotes tailored to team size, modules, and contract length. There are no self-serve plans or published rate cards on the CB Insights website.
Third-party procurement data from Vendr suggests annual contracts typically range from roughly $24,748 to $184,986, depending on scope. Smaller single-product engagements tend to land toward the lower end; enterprise deployments with API access, CRM integrations, and broad seat counts sit at the higher end.
To get pricing, request a demo or quote directly from CB Insights. Having competing quotes from PitchBook, Crunchbase Enterprise, or AlphaSense before the conversation can improve negotiating position.

CB Insights Pricing FAQ 
 01 How much does CB Insights cost? 
CB Insights does not list prices publicly. Contracts are quoted by sales based on which products you need (Browser Analyst, Strategy Terminal, Data Solutions), the number of seats, and contract length. Third-party procurement data from Vendr places typical annual contract values between roughly $24,748 and $184,986. Contact CB Insights directly for an accurate quote. 
 02 What is the cheapest CB Insights plan? 
CB Insights does not offer a publicly available entry-level plan with a set price. All tiers — Browser Analyst, Strategy Terminal, and Data Solutions — require a sales conversation. Based on third-party Vendr data, the lower end of reported contracts starts around $24,748 per year, though your actual quote will depend on seats and modules selected. 
 03 Does CB Insights have a free plan or free trial? 
CB Insights offers a limited free trial that surfaces a subset of company profiles and research previews without a credit card. Full platform access — including Mosaic Scores, ChatCBI, Market Maps, and Customer Transcripts — requires a paid subscription. Academic access may be available through select university partnerships. 
 04 How does CB Insights pricing compare to PitchBook or Crunchbase? 
CB Insights, PitchBook, and Crunchbase all use custom or tiered enterprise pricing that is not publicly disclosed in full. CB Insights focuses on predictive market intelligence and emerging tech trend analysis; PitchBook is the standard for raw deal flow, fund performance, and private market transactions; Crunchbase offers more accessible self-serve plans for basic company and funding data. For a direct cost comparison, request quotes from each vendor at the same time. 
 05 Are there multi-year discounts for CB Insights? 
Multi-year commitments (two or three years) are typically available and can reduce the per-year cost. Requesting a price cap to limit annual escalation at renewal is a common negotiation lever. CB Insights does not publish discount schedules, so any multi-year terms must be negotiated directly with their sales team. 
 06 Is there a free trial or free version of CB Insights? 
CB Insights does not offer a free tier or publicly listed free trial. Access is enterprise-only and requires contacting sales for a custom quote. 
 07 What do analysts like most about CB Insights? 
Users consistently praise CB Insights for timely funding round updates, data-driven strategic insights, and its ability to separate market hype from substantive signals. Reviewers highlight that it stays ahead of market changes better than competing services. 
 08 What are the main complaints about CB Insights data coverage? 
The most common complaints are limited company coverage (users want more companies indexed) and incomplete pre- and post-money valuation data for funding rounds. 
 09 Which CB Insights plan is right for my team? 
CB Insights offers three custom-priced plans: CB Insights Browser Analyst (designed for individual analyst access), CB Insights Strategy Terminal (for strategy and competitive intelligence teams), and CB Insights Data Solutions (for bulk data and API access). The right tier depends on your team's use case and data volume needs — contact CB Insights sales for a tailored quote.
