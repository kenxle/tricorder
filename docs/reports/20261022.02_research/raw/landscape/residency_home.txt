the residency exists to support and connect ambitious builders 
traditionally, ambitious builders like yourself have been constrained by the time requirements of school and work.
we run 3 month-long cohorts so you can go full time on your ambitions. we provide housing, co-working space, and support so you can live and work with equally ambitious peers.
you can think of us as “college” meets “accelerator”.
live with global talent
silicon valley is our first hub because of its unmatched talent density. but many bright minds cannot live here due to visa restrictions, high costs, and/or family obligations.
with residencies all over the world, we support those individuals wherever they are, connecting the most capable talent globally. 
What to expect 
use demo day to prove your skills to potential collaborators, employees, and investors - like a16z and 1517 fund.
stay clear minded and accountable with weekly syncs and coaching sessions.
connect with someone who will 10x your trajectory through intros and fireside chats - with people like sam altman, kevin hartz, and danielle strachman.
most of all, expect spontaneous adventures and late night conversations with your fellow residents... living the joy of human connection is what really makes the residency  ❤

questions?
are there any perks?
yes! lots of them. residents get compute credits, food discounts, and even free therapy!
do I need to be a founder?
we are looking for people who like to invent. people who bring novel ideas into the real world. this type of person tends to be a startup founder or researcher.
how long is the program?
it depends on the home and cohort you join! typically cohorts range between 3 and 6 months.
do you help with visas?
we can point you in the right direction
can I do this online?
no - the majority of this experience is in person because we strongly value human connection.
can I be in school or have a job?
unfortunately not. we want you to be able to go full time on your dreams! the residency is a time to be surrounded by people going all in.
where do we work?
in our co-working space or in our house. it depends on which location you are assigned to.
do I have my own room?
yes. we also have shared rooms for those looking to save on cost.
what about food?
food coordination varies per residency location. we have everything from chef-prepared meals to entirely resident-run kitchens. but at all residencies, healthy food over shared meals is enjoyed.
