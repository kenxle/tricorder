Introduction

Affinity is a relationship intelligence platform designed for deal-driven teams in venture capital, private equity, investment banking, and corporate development. Unlike traditional CRMs, Affinity automatically captures relationship data from email, calendar, and other sources to surface network insights, track deal flow, and manage pipelines. Pricing is based on user seats, feature tier, and contract term, with significant variation depending on team size and negotiation approach.

How much does Affinity cost in 2026?

Affinity uses a per-user, per-month pricing model with three main tiers: Essential, Advanced, and Enterprise. List pricing is not publicly disclosed, and Affinity typically requires direct sales engagement for quotes. Pricing varies based on user count, tier selection, contract term (annual vs. multi-year), and negotiation.

Based on anonymized Affinity transactions in Vendr's dataset, teams should expect:

  • Small teams (5–15 users): Annual contract values typically range from $15,000 to $45,000, depending on tier and negotiation outcomes.
  • Mid-sized teams (15–50 users): Annual contract values commonly fall between $45,000 and $150,000.
  • Larger deployments (50+ users): Annual contract values often exceed $150,000, with enterprise-tier pricing and volume-based negotiation playing a significant role.

Discounting is common, particularly for multi-year commitments, annual prepayment, and competitive evaluation scenarios. Vendr data shows buyers often achieve 15–30% below initial quotes through structured negotiation.

What actually drives Affinity costs?

Understanding the key cost drivers helps buyers model total cost accurately and identify negotiation opportunities.

User count and seat-based pricing

Affinity charges per user per month, making seat count the primary cost driver. Volume-based discounting is common for larger deployments, with per-seat pricing often decreasing as user count increases.

Vendr insight:

Buyers with 30+ users often achieve meaningfully lower per-seat pricing through volume-based negotiation, particularly when committing to multi-year terms.

Tier and feature selection

Moving from Essential to Advanced or Enterprise increases per-seat pricing significantly. Buyers should carefully assess which features are required versus aspirational to avoid over-purchasing.

Vendr insight:

Teams that start with Essential and expand later often pay higher effective rates than those who negotiate a multi-tier or growth-path agreement upfront.

Contract term length

Affinity strongly incentivizes multi-year commitments through discounting. Annual contracts are available but typically carry higher per-seat pricing.

Vendr insight:

Based on Vendr transaction data, buyers committing to 2- or 3-year terms often achieve 10–20% lower annual pricing compared to 1-year agreements.

Payment terms

Annual prepayment is standard, but Affinity may offer additional discounting for upfront payment versus quarterly or monthly billing.

Vendr insight:

Buyers who prepay annually often secure an additional 5–10% discount compared to quarterly payment schedules.

Add-ons and professional services

Onboarding, training, and custom integrations are often quoted separately and can add 10–25% to total first-year costs.

What hidden costs and fees should you plan for with Affinity?

Beyond base subscription pricing, buyers should budget for several additional cost categories that are often quoted separately or emerge during implementation.

Onboarding and implementation fees

Affinity typically charges for onboarding, data migration, and initial training. These fees vary based on team size and complexity but commonly range from $5,000 to $25,000 for mid-sized deployments.

Vendr insight:

Onboarding fees are often negotiable, particularly for larger contracts or competitive scenarios. Buyers can sometimes secure reduced or waived onboarding as part of the overall deal.

Training and enablement

Ongoing training, admin workshops, and user enablement sessions may be charged separately, particularly for Enterprise-tier customers with complex workflows.

Integration and API costs

While Affinity includes standard integrations, custom API work or third-party integration support may incur additional fees or require professional services engagements.

Data migration and cleanup

Migrating data from legacy CRMs or spreadsheets often requires professional services support, which can add $3,000 to $15,000 depending on data volume and complexity.

Support and success packages

Premium support, dedicated customer success managers, and SLA guarantees are typically reserved for Enterprise-tier customers and may be priced separately or bundled into higher-tier contracts.

Vendr insight:

Buyers should clarify what level of support is included in base pricing and negotiate any premium support packages before signing.

How do you negotiate Affinity pricing?

Affinity pricing is highly negotiable, particularly for buyers who engage early, demonstrate competitive evaluation, and anchor to budget constraints. These insights are based on anonymized Affinity deals in Vendr's dataset across a wide range of company sizes and contract structures.

1. Engage early and establish timeline pressure

Affinity's sales team is more flexible when buyers engage 60–90 days before a decision deadline. Early engagement allows time for competitive evaluation and creates natural leverage as the vendor works to close the deal within their fiscal quarter.

Vendr insight:

Buyers who engage near Affinity's quarter-end (March, June, September, December) often achieve better pricing as sales teams work to meet quotas.

2. Anchor to budget, not to list pricing

Rather than negotiating down from Affinity's initial quote, anchor the conversation to your budget and internal approval thresholds. Frame pricing discussions around what you can justify internally, not what the vendor wants to charge.

Example framing: "Our budget for this category is $X annually. If Affinity can work within that range, we're ready to move forward. Otherwise, we'll need to evaluate alternatives."

3. Leverage competitive alternatives

Affinity competes with Salesforce, HubSpot, Attio, and other CRMs. Demonstrating active evaluation of alternatives—particularly lower-cost options like Attio or feature-rich platforms like Salesforce—creates pricing pressure.

Competitive benchmarks: Compare Affinity pricing to alternatives to understand how Affinity's pricing stacks up against similar platforms for your requirements.

4. Commit to multi-year terms for deeper discounts

Affinity strongly incentivizes 2- and 3-year commitments through discounting. Buyers willing to commit to longer terms often achieve 10–20% lower annual pricing compared to 1-year agreements.

Vendr insight:

Multi-year deals also provide pricing predictability and reduce the risk of renewal price increases.

5. Negotiate onboarding and professional services

Onboarding fees are often quoted separately and are negotiable. Buyers can sometimes secure reduced or waived onboarding as part of the overall deal, particularly for larger contracts.

6. Clarify renewal terms and price escalation

Affinity contracts often include auto-renewal clauses and annual price escalation (typically 5–10%). Negotiate caps on renewal price increases and ensure you have adequate notice periods before auto-renewal.

Vendr insight:

Buyers who negotiate renewal terms upfront avoid surprise price increases and maintain leverage for future renewals.

Product FAQs

What's the difference between Affinity Essential, Advanced, and Enterprise?

  • Essential: Core CRM functionality, automated data capture, basic reporting. Designed for small teams beginning to formalize relationship intelligence.
  • Advanced: Adds workflow automation, advanced integrations, custom fields, and enhanced reporting. Designed for growing teams needing deeper customization.
  • Enterprise: Adds advanced security controls, dedicated support, custom onboarding, API access, and enterprise-grade integrations. Designed for large teams with complex workflows and compliance requirements.

Does Affinity offer a free trial?

Affinity does not typically offer a free trial but may provide demo environments or proof-of-concept engagements for qualified buyers. Contact Affinity's sales team to discuss trial options.

Can I add users mid-contract?

Yes, Affinity allows mid-contract user additions, typically prorated to the remaining contract term. Pricing for additional users is often based on the original per-seat rate, though buyers can sometimes negotiate volume-based discounting for larger expansions.

What integrations does Affinity support?

Affinity integrates with email providers (Gmail, Outlook), calendar systems, Slack, Salesforce, and other common business tools. Advanced and Enterprise tiers include API access for custom integrations.
