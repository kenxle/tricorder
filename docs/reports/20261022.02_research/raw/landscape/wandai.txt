A structural change
in what intelligence can do
When software becomes a worker, the organization changes.
So does the economy around it.
A worker carries a responsibility forward. It recognizes new work, develops a plan, acts within its authority, and checks the result. It has an identity, an operating history, and experience it can use again. Those properties turn a capable model into a continuing contributor.
That new source of labor needs somewhere to belong. People and agents need shared roles, objectives, processes, and governance. Organizations need to coordinate them. Nations need the infrastructure and institutions through which their work can become productive capacity.
