Frequently Asked Questions
What does AI actually do inside a private capital CRM?
Three things worth the name. It scores relationship strength from communication patterns, which no rules engine does well. It extracts structured facts from unstructured sources such as meeting notes and founder updates. And it drafts briefs, follow-ups, and summaries from the firm's own records. Everything else marketed as CRM AI is usually search with a new label.
What should a firm ask a CRM vendor about AI?
Whether the AI runs on the firm's own data or on a general model with no access to it, since the two produce very different answers. Whether outputs are reviewable before they change a record. Whether AI features cost extra. And whether the firm's data is used to train models. The answer should be no, in writing.
What does Affinity use AI for?
Affinity uses AI to score relationship strength from communication patterns, extract deal signals and field updates from meeting notes, rank warm introduction paths through the firm's network, and draft briefs and follow-ups. Each output is grounded in the firm's own CRM data rather than a general model's view of the market.
How does Affinity apply AI to deal sourcing?
Affinity builds a relationship graph from captured email and calendar activity, then ranks warm introduction paths to a named target by connection strength. Because scoring runs on the firm's own interaction history rather than a generic model, the paths surfaced reflect who the team actually knows, which is what moved founder interactions at Uncork Capital up 45%.
Which AI capabilities come with each Affinity plan?
Affinity includes relationship scoring, SmartSearch, and Affinity Labs from the Essential plan. Notetaker, AI Chat, Ascend agents, and the hosted MCP server are included from Scale up. Advanced adds Company Growth Insights and the third-party enrichment the AI draws on. No tier charges a separate add-on fee for AI.
How much time does Affinity's AI save a deal team?
Affinity doesn't publish a time-to-close figure, and the honest gains come earlier in the process. Automatic capture removes logging, agents assemble briefs and draft follow-ups, and deal teams recover 180+ hours per person annually. Most of that time returns to the parts of the job that need judgment.
Is a firm's data used to train Affinity's AI models?
No. Customer data is not used to train models shared across firms. AI features operate on each firm's own data within its own environment, governed by the same permission sets that control the rest of the CRM. The deployment is isolated per customer, encrypted in transit and at rest, and independently certified.
