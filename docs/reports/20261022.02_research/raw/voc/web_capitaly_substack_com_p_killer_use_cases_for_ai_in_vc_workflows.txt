 Transcript

 AI isn't just hype—it’s quietly reshaping how top VCs work.
 If you’re in venture and still doing everything manually, you're already behind. The smartest firms and solo GPs are embedding AI into every layer of their workflows—from cold outreach to portfolio tracking.
 Here’s a breakdown of real, practical AI use cases from top investors (yes, actually in the trenches). Let's map them out.
 🔍 Deal Sourcing 

 Social listening for traction signals : Tools like Poach VC and Specter let you spot breakout companies before they trend on Twitter. 

 CRM enrichment with 10-K data : Some VCs are using AI agents to scan filings and auto-personalize outreach—M&A-ready. 

 Signal-based sourcing : Using AI to track news, funding announcements, team hires, or GitHub activity to find stealthy companies. 

 👉 Want to scale outbound to founders fast? Use these 15 best cold email templates for investor outreach . 
 🤝 Founder Outreach + Personalization 

 Apollo sequences with AI assist : Plug ChatGPT directly into sequences to auto-personalize each step based on founder data. 

 Tone tuning : Modify the voice of cold emails based on recipient persona. 

 Meeting prep : Use tools like Claude or Perplexity to gather deep insights fast before calls. 

 📞 Founder Call Summaries 

 Transcription + structuring : Whisper transcribes the call. GPT summarizes it by section (product, team, traction, fundraise, etc.). 

 Auto-sync to Affinity : Structured summaries are uploaded into CRM. No manual entry. 

 📄 Deal Memos + Diligence 

 Drafting support : Use ChatGPT to create first-draft deal memos based on call notes, LinkedIn bios, market data. 

 Thematic research : Use Perplexity for deep, context-aware market analysis in seconds. 

 📊 Portfolio Support 

 Investor updates : AI tools can turn founder notes into clean updates. 

 Modeling scenarios : Claude or GPT-4 can model out various revenue inflection paths. 

 Task and project summaries : AI-enhanced notes tools integrate into CRMs to show action items. 

 🧠 High-Leverage Tools To Explore 

 Yohei Nakajima : Built BabyAGI and documented several VC-AI use cases. 

 Lovable : Rapid idea validation tool. 

 n8n : No-code automation for VCs with deep integrations. 

 What Other VCs Said 

 “Claude helps me model revenue paths.”

 “Social listening finds products before Twitter hypes them.”

 “We summarize founder calls, slice insights by topic, and sync to CRM—automatically.”

 “Signal is our go-to sourcing tool for Europe.”

 FAQs 

 What’s the most common use case of AI in VC today? 
 Deal sourcing and call summarization are where most funds are starting. 
 Is it too early to rely on AI for diligence? 
 Not entirely—but use it for prep, not decisions. 
 Which tools are worth trying? 
 Whisper, Affinity, Apollo, Perplexity, Claude, GPT-4, Lovable, Specter. 
 Is this replacing analysts? 
 No—but it's making junior roles 10x more efficient. 
 Final Take 

 Venture workflows are about speed, precision, and leverage.
 AI gives you all three—if you use it right.
 The best investors are already embedding GPT, Whisper, and Claude deep into sourcing, screening, and supporting.
