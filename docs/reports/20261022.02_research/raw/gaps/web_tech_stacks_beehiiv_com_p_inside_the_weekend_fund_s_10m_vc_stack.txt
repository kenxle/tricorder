 Inside the Weekend Fund’s $10M VC Stack
 How Ryan Hoover runs a lean, founder-friendly fund with tools like Notion, Zapier, Streak, and Slack

 Mark Delaney 
 September 13, 2025 

 ✨Hey there, tech stackers! 🚀 

 Today we’re stepping inside the world of Weekend Fund , a $10M early-stage fund run by Ryan Hoover (yes, the founder of Product Hunt ). What’s striking? They run lean, automate intelligently, and use no-fluff tools to keep the fund personal and scalable. 

 Whether you're building a fund, running ops solo, or supporting a lean investing team—this is a minimalist stack worth studying. 

 💼 The Weekend Fund Stack (aka How to Run a $10M Fund Like a Startup)

 This isn't a bloated ops machine. It's a thoughtfully selected system built for transparency, speed, and collaboration. 

 📬 Email & CRM

 Gmail 
Still the center of fund communication, integrated tightly with almost everything. 

 Streak 
A lightweight CRM that lives inside Gmail. Used to track LP convos, founder relationships, and internal workflows without switching tools. 

 📁 Docs & Collaboration

 Notion 
The brain of the fund: memos, deal tracking, LP reports, internal processes, FAQs, etc. 

 Google Drive 
Still their preferred place for sharing decks and spreadsheets. 

 Loom 
Used for async founder updates and internal walkthroughs. Keeps team members aligned across time zones. 

 🤝 Community & Founder Support

 Slack 
The Weekend Fund runs a private Slack for founders. The channel is a hub for intros, templates, AMAs, and support requests. 

 Zapier 
Automates form entries, tracks dealflow, routes submissions, and logs tasks into Notion. Invisible glue that saves dozens of hours. 

 🧮 Finance & Reporting

 Carta 
Manages cap tables, SAFEs, and equity positions across portfolio companies. 

 Pulley 
Used by some portfolio companies for founder cap table management and fundraising prep. 

 AngelList 
Their back-office for fund admin—distributions, capital calls, LP management. 

 ✨ Founder Tools They Recommend

 Beyond their own stack, the Weekend Fund actively recommends tools to founders: 

 Tally   – Form builder 

 Canva   – Design 

 Typefully   – Social media writing 

 Superhuman   – Email power-user tool 

 Cal.com – Scheduling 

 Linear   – Issue tracking 

 💡 What Makes This Stack Work?

 🧠  Centralized Knowledge (Notion + Zapier)
Everything is documented. When something breaks or needs onboarding, it’s already written up. 

 📨  Founder-First Comms (Slack + Loom)
Helps founders get support without long email chains. 

 🔌  Automation Everywhere 
From deal intake to LP onboarding, they cut friction with smart zaps and embedded workflows.
