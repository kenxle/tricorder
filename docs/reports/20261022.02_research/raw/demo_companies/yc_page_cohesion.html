Cohesion: Your public equities agentic teammate
See original launch post

Cohesion is your public equities agentic teammate.

Cohesion agents track earnings, news, and non-traditional datasets like podcasts and X/Twitter.

Today’s tools require querying, endless reading, and manual search across decade-old platforms that aren’t capturing the most important datasets.

We built a team of AI agents that monitor the companies you cover and generate differentiated insights. The result? Captured alpha, less time searching “why is $stock down”, automated idea generation - all in a system purpose-built for public equity investing that doesn't require manual setup.

https://youtu.be/DZMmSVwp2Pk

Today, Cohesion is live with 10+ long/short and long-only fundamental equity funds managing a combined $10Bn+ AUM. Built by a team with 10+ years of public markets investing experience.

In advance of the upcoming earnings season, we’re offering limited early trial access. Take 2 minutes to send 20 tickers and have the easiest earnings season of your career: https://cohesionplatform.com/#start-free-trial
