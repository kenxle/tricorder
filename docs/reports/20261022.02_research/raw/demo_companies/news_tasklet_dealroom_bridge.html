Tasklet raises $13M bridge round for AI agents, backed by USV and Lightspeed

What's the deal? Tasklet, a San Francisco-based AI startup, has raised a $13 million pre-Series A bridge round led by Union Square Ventures and Lightspeed Venture Partners. What's the endgame? Founded by Andrew Lee and Jonny Dimond, Tasklet runs a cloud-based platform that lets companies delegate daily workflows to AI agents. The agents connect to a company's software and data sources and autonomously carry out research, prepare reports, update CRM systems, classify emails, and process data.

Running continuously in the cloud, the agents take responsibility for ongoing workflows rather than one-off tasks. Tasklet now aims to build enterprise AI infrastructure, where teams manage agents, corporate knowledge, app connections, and business processes in a shared environment.

The numbers: Tasklet recorded over 1,200% growth in the first four months of 2026. Its previous round was raised at a $175 million valuation; the valuation for the bridge was not disclosed.

Who's behind it? CEO Lee previously co-founded FirebaseDealroom has a profile for this one. Try Dealroom →, which Google acquired. Dimond was on Firebase's early team and later led Google Cloud Firestore technically. The wider team draws from Firebase, Google, Amazon, and OpenAI.

Oleka Capital managing partner Ilker Sozdinler said the firm first encountered Tasklet in January 2026 as a case study at Harvard Business School. "The best investment theses emerge not in an Excel file but in daily usage habits," he said, adding that the Oleka team put Tasklet "at the center of our own daily workflows."

The signal: Investor appetite for AI agents that can run enterprise workflows continues to draw major funds, and Tasklet's founder pedigree and steep early growth make it a test case for whether agent tooling can graduate into core business infrastructure.
