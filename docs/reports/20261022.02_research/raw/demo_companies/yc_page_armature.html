We get your product picked by coding agents.

Coding agents like Claude Code, Codex and Cursor now decide which tools get installed in a codebase. Armature runs the real agents inside a panel of realistic repositories, measures whether they pick you, and works with your team on the docs, SDK and content that make them choose you. We call this Agent-Led Growth (~coding agents GEO for dev tools). And once agents use your product, Armature shows you what users do with it through AI, with self-serve MCP analytics and evals that catch regressions before your users see them.

Company Launches
Armature - Making your app finally usable by AI agents
See original launch post

Hey everyone, we’re Theodore and Louis - Co-Founders of Armature 👋

TL;DR

Armature helps companies monitor, analyze, and optimize how AI agents experience their products.

  • We discover your product through MCP and CLI, run the real workflows your customers’ AI agents do and let you finally see your customers’ agents thinking traces.
  • We provide analytics, run benchmarks, and turn this into concrete actions to improve your MCP and CLI.
  • This is only the beginning: we are reinventing the building blocks of the User Agent Experience.

The Ask: If you are building a product that is increasingly used by AI agents or if you want to ensure agents can use your product but don’t know where to start, we’d love to hear about your challenges! You can also try our product self-serve for free directly at armature.tech.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3Gtc5RzGxJ0

❌ The Problem: The Missing Agent Experience (AX)

We have 30 years of tooling to optimize User Experience (UX), from session replays and funnel analytics to live support chat. But as products are increasingly operated by AI agents rather than humans, we lack the equivalent tooling for Agent Experience (AX).

Right now, companies have zero visibility into how each agent (Claude, Codex, ChatGPT, OpenClaw, etc.) interacts with their products. And when a bad AX causes an agent to fail silently, the end-user simply churns.

But it goes deeper. As agents become the primary users, the fundamental mechanics of software products simply stop working. Because if a human isn’t looking at the screen:

  • How do you upsell a premium feature to an AI?
  • How do you build brand loyalty with a user that doesn’t feel emotions?
  • How do you offer customer support when an agent fails?

We are building Armature to solve these exact problems and so many more questions related to this shift!

💡 Our Solution: Armature

Armature helps companies monitor, analyze, and optimize how AI agents experience their products.

What we do today:

  • Automated testing: We run complex sessions against your MCPs/CLIs across all major harnesses and models to catch broken workflows before your users do.
  • AX analytics & benchmarking: No more black boxes. We trace agent sessions to show you exactly what the model "thought", what context it missed, and why it failed. We measure your AX against competitors to ensure you remain the preferred platform for agents to use.
  • Auto-remediation: Our MCP lets your internal coding agents plug into Armature to autonomously fix your product's AX based on our run data.

Our vision for tomorrow:

Reaching full understanding of how agents use your product and how you can improve it is only step one.

The next step is to provide the foundational infrastructure for the Agent Experience, powering agent-native revenue, lifecycle marketing, and customer support for the next generation of software.

🙏 Our Ask: If you’re building apps for agents, let’s talk!

  1. Audit your MCP or CLI: If you are building for agents, you can start by running your first audit & benchmark on armature.tech for free to see how they actually experience your app.
  2. Let’s chat: If you are facing challenges to make your app usable by agents or optimize the agent experience, please reach out to founders@armature.tech. We’d love to discuss and help you with this!

👬 The Team: why we are working on this

Before founding Armature, Louis scaled AI agents at Joko, a 6M user consumer app. Theo was a FDE at Palantir and then built petabytes-scale observability from scratch as a Founding Engineer at Tsuga, a Datadog spin-off.

Blindly shipping a self-tested MCP to 6M of edge cases drove us crazy so we decided to build the solution.
