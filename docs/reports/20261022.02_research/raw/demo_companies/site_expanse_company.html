Our Mission

As AI workloads continue to grow in complexity, infrastructure teams are forced to estimate compute requirements with limited visibility.

These assumptions often lead to wasted resources, failed jobs, and unnecessary costs. Expanse exists to eliminate that uncertainty by giving teams predictive insights before execution and actionable diagnostics afterward, making infrastructure decisions based on evidence rather than guesswork.
