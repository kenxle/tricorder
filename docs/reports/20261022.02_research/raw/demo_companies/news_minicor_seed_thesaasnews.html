Minicor Raises $3.8M Seed

Minicor raises $3.8M seed round led by IA Ventures to help enterprises integrate modern AI agents with legacy desktop business software systems.

by Ben Murray
Updated October 15, 2026

Minicor Raises $3.8M Seed

Minicor, a San Francisco-based AI startup, has raised $3.8 million in seed funding to support its automation infrastructure platform. The company provides a solution that allows modern AI applications to connect with and control legacy desktop business software that lacks traditional APIs.

Investors

The funding round was led by Brad Gillespie of IA Ventures. Additional participating investors included Y Combinator, Paul Graham, Pioneer Fund, Robinhood Ventures, Garage Capital, Rebel Fund, K5 Global, and 20VC.

Minicor Use of Funds

Minicor plans to use the capital to further develop its automation infrastructure, which enables businesses to adopt AI without needing to replace existing legacy software systems. The funds will support the platform's ability to turn desktop workflows into API endpoints and refine its AI agent technology.

About Minicor

Founded by Faizaan Chishtie and Saheed Akinbile, Minicor aims to solve the challenge of deploying AI within established enterprise environments. Their product allows AI agents to interact with traditional desktop applications, helping companies in industries such as healthcare, financial services, automotive services, and logistics to automate workflows and integrate modern AI with legacy operations.
