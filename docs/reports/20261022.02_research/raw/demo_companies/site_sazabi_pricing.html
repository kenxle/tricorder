Pricing, explained

Understand your subscription, included usage, and what goes into your bill.

Read docs

Hobby, Startup, and Growth each include 200 GB of logs, 50 GB of logs scanned, 20 issues, and 5,000 automation runs per cycle. Additional logs ingested and logs scanned cost $0.10 per GB, additional issues cost $4 each, and additional automation runs cost $0.01 each. Each plan also includes 20 issue rejections per cycle.
