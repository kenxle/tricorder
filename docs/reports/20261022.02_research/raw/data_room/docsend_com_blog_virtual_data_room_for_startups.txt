 Checklist of what to include in a startup data room for investors 
 In addition to the essential features we list above, there are a few other essential things you should include in your startup data room to get the result you want:
 - Profit and loss statements, balance sheets, cash flow statements

- Detailed description of the business model, market analysis, and growth strategy

- Articles of incorporation, licenses, patents, or other legal documents

- Bios and resumes of executives, founders, major shareholders, and key personnel

- Details on product descriptions, prototypes, patents, and market research

- Pitch decks outlining the business opportunity, market, and financial projections
