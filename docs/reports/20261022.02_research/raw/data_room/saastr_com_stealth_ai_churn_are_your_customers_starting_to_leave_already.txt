 Stealth AI Churn: Are Your Customers Starting to Leave Already?

 by Jason Lemkin | Artificial Intelligence (AI) , Blog Posts , SaaStr.Ai 

 Stealth AI Churn: Are Your Customers Starting to Leave Already?

 The churn signals are everywhere if you know where to look.

 In some segments, you can see it all over the place:

- In the contact center, the vendor musical chairs have already begun in the Age of AI.  None of the products are remotely the same as they were 12 months ago, from Fin to Gorgias to Dialpad to Zendesk to Talkdesk.

- Dev tools companies are watching customers rapidly go all-in on AI-first alternatives.

- But here’s what’s really interesting—and potentially more dangerous—this “stealth churn” is happening in categories that seem more resilient to AI Change.

 Take Canva . We’ve been happy, paying customers at SaaStr for 8+ years. Love the product. It’s cheap, it works, we’re not going anywhere. Classic sticky B2B customer.

 Except … we’re also now using:

- Reve Art for thumbnails and most images

- Higgsfield for short videos

- Opus Pro for clips

- Descript for our podcast editing

- Eleven Labs for audio

- Gamma for many slides

 We haven’t churned from Canva. We probably never will. But we do use it significantly less than we did 18 months ago. Our DAUs / MAUs are down . Our feature usage is down.  Because we are using AI tools to do more.  And when renewal time comes, that upsell conversation just got a lot harder.

 This is stealth AI churn, and it’s coming for every B2B category.

 We stealth-churned off Notion months ago.

 We didn't realize it. We didn't tell Notion.

 It's just … our agents have no need for it. They're doing the work now.

 Stealth churn is the under-discussed agent risk. Usage drops to zero quietly. The renewal email never even goes…

 The New Churn Math

 Traditional churn is binary: customer pays or doesn’t pay. Stealth churn is analog: customers pay the same for now — but use you 40% less. They’re still showing up in your retention cohorts, still generating ARR, still appearing in your success metrics.

 But the underlying consumption patterns tell a different story:

- Session frequency drops as AI tools handle quick tasks

- Feature adoption stalls because alternatives exist for advanced use cases

- Time-to-value shrinks but so does total time spent

- Expansion revenue evaporates as customers find they need less, not more

 The scary part? Your NPS might actually go up. Customers love using you for what you’re genuinely best at, while AI handles the rest. They’re happier with a smaller slice of their workflow.

 The Canva Case Study in Every Category

 This isn’t just happening to design tools. Look around:

 Customer Support : Teams still use Zendesk for complex tickets but route simple queries to AI chatbots.  Same CSM relationship, half the ticket volume.

 Sales Tools : Reps still love their CRM but use AI for email sequences, call summaries, and lead research. The core platform stays, the time spent shrinks.

 Project Management : Teams keep their Monday or Asana workspace but use AI for task breakdowns, status updates, and resource planning.

 Marketing Automation : The main platform handles campaigns, but AI writes the copy, generates the creatives, and optimizes the send times.

 In each case, the primary vendor keeps the relationship but loses mindshare, usage depth, and expansion opportunity.

 Why This Matters More Than Hard Churn

 Hard churn is obvious and immediate. You lose $50K ARR, you know it instantly. Stealth churn is insidious:

- Leading indicators lag : Usage drops months before renewal impact shows up

- Expansion dies first : Why upgrade when you need less functionality?

- Switching costs erode : Less usage means less data lock-in

- Competitive moats narrow : AI tools commoditize your differentiated features

 By the time stealth churn shows up in your revenue metrics, the damage is baked in.

 The Three Types of AI Displacement

 Not all AI threats are created equal. We’re seeing three distinct patterns:

 Task Displacement : AI handles specific jobs within your workflow (AI writes the first draft, customers polish in your tool)

 Feature Displacement : AI replicates your advanced features with better UX (why learn complex automation when AI just does it?)

 Workflow Displacement : AI creates entirely new workflows that bypass your category (why design in a tool when AI generates the final asset?)

 Canva faces all three. AI generates thumbnails (task), creates complex designs (feature), and produces finished videos (workflow).

 The Stealth Churn Playbook

 If you’re seeing usage metrics softening while retention holds steady, here’s your action plan:

 Immediate (0-30 days) :

- Segment customers by usage change patterns, not just retention

- Survey high-value customers about their AI tool adoption

- Map which features/workflows AI tools are replacing

- Identify early adopters who might signal broader trends

 Short-term (1-6 months) :

- Build AI capabilities into your core workflows rather than separate features

- Focus expansion conversations on AI-resistant use cases

- Double down on collaborative/multi-user features that AI can’t replicate

- Create AI integration partnerships instead of competing head-to-head

 Long-term (6+ months) :

- Evolve from tool to platform—become the place where AI outputs get refined, approved, and managed

- Shift value prop from “we do X” to “we help you do X better/faster/at scale”

- Build switching costs around data, integrations, and team workflows

- Consider acquiring AI-first competitors in adjacent use cases

 They May Still Love You — But Also Start Using the AI Best-of-Breed Players

 Your customers aren’t lying when they say they love your product. They’re not plotting to leave. They’re just gradually needing you less.

 And in a world where “doing more with less” is every company’s mantra, that gradual decrease in need becomes a gradual decrease in budget allocation.

 The companies that survive the AI transition won’t be the ones that build the best AI features. They’ll be the ones that evolve their entire value proposition around being indispensable orchestrators of AI-human workflows.

 Because in the end, stealth churn isn’t about losing customers. It’s about becoming less essential to the customers you keep.
