What is a veterinary answering service?

A veterinary answering service answers calls for animal hospitals and vet clinics when your team can't, after hours, during overflow, or on weekends. Live agents take messages, capture new-client details, schedule appointments, and escalate true emergencies to your on-call veterinarian following your protocols.

Do veterinary answering services handle after-hours emergencies?

Yes. A veterinary answering service provides 24/7 coverage and routes urgent calls, like a pet in distress or a livestock emergency, straight to your on-call vet, while routine calls are logged as messages for the next business day.

How does a veterinary answering service work?

When a call comes in, a live US-based agent answers in your clinic's name, follows your script to screen the call, and either patches an emergency to your on-call team or takes a message. Everything is logged so your staff has the details when they need them.
