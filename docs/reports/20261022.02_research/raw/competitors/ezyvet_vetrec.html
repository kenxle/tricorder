About VetRec

VetRec is a veterinary AI company purpose-built to reduce the documentation burden on veterinary professionals. Its flagship product uses advanced speech recognition and large language models trained specifically on veterinary clinical language to generate accurate,review-ready SOAP notes from patient encounters — directly integrated into the practice's PIMS.

VetRec was founded with the mission of giving vets their time back, addressing one of the leading drivers of burnout in veterinary medicine: excessive administrative workload. The company serves general practice veterinary clinics across the United States (and other supported regions), helping practices improve efficiency, staff retention, and quality of care through intelligent automation. VetRec is committed to building technology that works the way veterinary teams actually work — fast, accurate, and without friction.

ezyVet & VetRec

VetRec integrates directly with ezyVet to streamline clinical documentation for veterinary practices. During a patient encounter, VetRec's AI listens and generates a structured SOAP note in real time, which is then automatically populated into the corresponding ezyVet patient record — eliminating the need for vets to manually type or transcribe notes after each appointment.

The integration is configured at the practice level and requires noongoing technical maintenance, making it accessible to practices of all sizes. By connecting VetRec's AI documentation engine with ezyVet's practice management workflows, veterinary teams can reduce time spent on administrative tasks, improve records accuracy, and increase appointment capacity without adding staff.

For more information, see the VetRec + ezyVet integration documentation.
