# Diligence interviews

Ken's interviews before Saturday, as the organisers ask every team to do. One section per person. Role only, no names, so this file can be read by the team.

The organisers' three questions, from [the problem tracks](../20261022.01_hackathon_sources/problem_tracks.txt):

1. What do you check first when you open a data room?
2. Which red flag did you once miss and regret?
3. How long does your first-pass memo take, and which part is the slowest?

Two more the critics asked for, because the public research could not answer them ([research_voice_of_customer.md](research_voice_of_customer.md), "What this means for Tricorder"):

4. Have you ever caught churn hidden in a cohort chart, or burn that did not match the hiring plan?
5. What does your fund pay for today to do first-pass diligence, and who signs for it?

Two about the data room itself, from [what a data room is](what_is_a_data_room.md):

6. How do data rooms reach you today: a DocSend link, a Drive folder, attachments in the email?
7. At what point in your process do you ask for one, and what do you open first?

For angels, because the research has only two angels on what they pay for:

8. Would you pay for a better first pass of your own, or do you rely on a lead, a syndicate or YC to do it? What would a first pass be worth to you per deal?
