# Document guides

One guide per document a seed or Series A data room can hold. 33 guides, listed by group. Written from [the brief](00_brief.md); this index is built by [build_index.py](build_index.py).


## Customers

| Guide | What it is |
|---|---|
| [Customer contracts and order forms (top ten)](customer-contracts-and-order-forms.md) | A customer contract is the signed agreement between the company and one customer. |
| [Customer references and letters of intent](customer-references-and-letters-of-intent.md) | Two kinds of document sit under this name. |
| [Material agreements other than customer contracts](material-agreements-other-than-customer-contracts.md) | DocSend's checklist groups these as 'Material agreements' and lists property leases, loans and liens, loans the company has made, insurance policies, licences of the company's IP to others and of other people's IP to the company, standard forms, consulting agreements, agreements with officers and directors, and anything outside the ordinary course that could matter to the business. |

## Equity

| Guide | What it is |
|---|---|
| [409A valuation](409a-valuation.md) | A 409A valuation is an outside appraisal of what the company's common stock is worth. |
| [Board and stockholder minutes](board-stockholder-minutes.md) | The written record of the decisions the board and the stockholders make. |
| [Cap table, fully diluted, current and pro forma](cap-table.md) | A cap table lists every holder of the company's equity and what each one holds: common stock, preferred stock, options, warrants, SAFEs and notes. |
| [Disclosure of claims, disputes and regulatory inquiries](claims-disputes-inquiries.md) | A written statement from the company that lists, or says there are none of: - Pending or threatened lawsuits, demand letters and arbitration. |
| [IP and invention assignment agreements](ip-assignment.md) | An invention assignment agreement is the paper by which a person transfers to the company what they create for it. |
| [Incorporation documents and bylaws](incorporation-documents.md) | The formation set for a company: - The **certificate (or articles) of incorporation** as filed with the state, plus every amendment, or the restated version. |
| [Prior financing: SAFEs, notes, term sheets](prior-financing.md) | The papers for each earlier raise: - **SAFEs and convertible notes**, with their side letters (pro rata, most favored nation, information rights, "major investor" status). |

## Financials

| Guide | What it is |
|---|---|
| [Audited or reviewed financials](audited-or-reviewed-financials.md) | Financial statements that an outside CPA firm has worked on, with the firm's signed report on the front. |
| [Bank statements (3 to 12 months)](bank-statements.md) | Monthly statements for every business bank account, and often the company's cards, loans and payment-processor balances. |
| [Financial model, 3 to 5 years](financial-model.md) | The financial model is the founders' spreadsheet of revenue, costs, hiring and cash. |
| [Historical P&L, balance sheet, cash flow (monthly)](historical-financials.md) | Three statements, month by month, from the company's accounting system: - the profit and loss statement (revenue, cost of revenue, operating costs, net result); - the balance sheet (cash, receivables, deferred revenue, payables, equity); - the cash flow statement (cash in and out, split into operating, investing and financing). |
| [Tax returns (three years)](tax-returns.md) | The company's filed income tax returns, with proof of filing. |
| [Use of funds plan](use-of-funds-plan.md) | A short plan for the money in this round: how much, spent on what, over how many months, to reach which milestones. |

## Metrics

| Guide | What it is |
|---|---|
| [Cohort or retention export](cohort-or-retention-export.md) | A table that groups customers by the month they started and shows how many of them, or how much of their revenue, is left in each later month. |
| [Investor update archive](investor-update-archive.md) | The emails or PDFs a founder has sent to existing investors, usually monthly, usually with the same sections each time: headline numbers, wins, problems, and a few requests for help. |
| [KPI dashboard or metrics export (MRR, ARR, customers, churn)](kpi-dashboard-or-metrics-export.md) | A short table of company-level numbers by month: monthly recurring revenue (MRR), annual recurring revenue (ARR), paying customers, churn, and often cash and burn. |
| [Monthly revenue by customer](monthly-revenue-by-customer.md) | A table with one row per customer per month, showing what each customer paid or was billed. |
| [Sales pipeline snapshot](sales-pipeline-snapshot.md) | A dated list or chart of the deals the company is working: who the buyer is, the dollar value, the stage, when the deal was created, when it is expected to close, where it came from, and the next step. |
| [Unit economics (CAC, LTV, payback)](unit-economics.md) | A set of numbers that say whether the company makes money on the average customer after paying to win and serve them: - **CAC** (customer acquisition cost): what it costs to win one new paying customer. |

## Story

| Guide | What it is |
|---|---|
| [Cover note and document index](cover-note-and-document-index.md) | The cover note is a one-page letter that sits at the top of the data room. |
| [Founder video or recorded intro](founder-video-or-recorded-intro.md) | A short recording of the founders talking. |
| [Market sizing and competitive landscape](market-sizing-and-competitive-landscape.md) | Two parts that Tricorder's table treats as one document: - **Market sizing:** the founder's estimate of how much revenue the product could earn, in layers. |
| [One-page summary](one-page-summary.md) | A one-page (sometimes two-page) fact sheet that sums up the company: what it does, the team, traction, market, the ask and what was raised before. |
| [Pitch deck](pitch-deck.md) | The deck is a 10 to 20 slide presentation that tells the company's story and states its numbers. |
| [Product roadmap and demo](product-roadmap-and-demo.md) | Two things that Tricorder's table groups as one document: - **The roadmap:** a short plan of what the team will build and when. |
