 How do I write a great monthly investor update?

 Keep it short and consistent: top-line metrics, a couple of highlights, the honest lowlights, and one or two specific asks. Send it every single month, on time, whether the news is good or bad. The founders who report crisply and predictably are the ones investors go to bat for, because they've earned trust.
