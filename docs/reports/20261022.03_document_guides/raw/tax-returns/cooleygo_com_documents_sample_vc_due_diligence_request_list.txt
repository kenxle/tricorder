 Sample VC Due Diligence Request List

 This sample VC due diligence request list will help you understand what VCs will look for before they’ll close your financing. It may be helpful as a guide for organizing your corporate and legal records now – the sooner you get your corporate house in order, the easier it will be to maintain.

 Check out our article  Why You Can’t Afford To Be Disorganized  for more tips on getting and staying organized.
