Title: 4 Types of Documents Founders Must Have in Their Fundraising Data Room (and a Few Additional Tips). Gale Wilkinson, Founder and Managing Partner of Vitalize Venture Capital. October 14, 2021.

Lines as on the page:
"Financial documents are among the first files I look for in a data room."
"Founders should include historical profit-and-loss statements since inception as well as a year-to-date statement if it's the middle of the year. I also need to see the company's most recent balance sheet."
"Companies at the pre-seed stage should show 3 years of annual projections, and companies at the seed stage should show 5 years."
"These are fairly basic models that include revenue by segment, cost of sales, expenses, and EBITDA (a proxy for cash burn)."
"For example, how many salespeople are required to bring in the expected number of customers each year? Is enough being spent on R&D? Are gross margins reasonable?"
List of references: "Key members of the management team"; "1-2 customers"; "1-2 current investors (if any)"; "Advisors"; "Other people who know you well and who can strengthen the reason to believe in the business".
"a good strategy is to have your lead investor speak to these customer references, write up notes, and then share those in the data room."
Current and pro forma cap tables: "Founders need to share current and pro forma cap tables to show potential investors how equity in the business is currently allocated and how allocations will change after the present round of funding."
"Detailed cap tables are important because they give me insight into how the company's stakeholders might have shifted over time. For example, is there someone who owns equity but is no longer with the business?"
Investment documents: "If you're doing a SAFE you'll certainly need that document in your data room." Existing SAFEs or convertible notes that will convert in this round.
Other documents to consider: articles of incorporation; investors already committed to the round and amounts; "a detailed plan for the use of the funds from this round"; product roadmap; go-to-market strategy; "Any links to press coverage of the company"; "Usage data from beta testing, pilots, or early customers"; "Customer list"; "Sample customer contract"; "Patents or trademarks"; outstanding lawsuits.
"Aim for 4-7 sections in your data room"
"It should only take a few hours to put a data room together at the beginning of a fundraise."
