 Usage & pricing PAY FOR WHAT YOU USE
 Prices and included usage are per 30-day cycle. Hobby, Startup, and Growth include the same usage allowances and rates; workspace limits and features vary by plan. Enterprise pricing is custom. No yearly billing or discount.
 Issues surfaced Problems Sazabi identifies from your logs. Hobby, Startup, and Growth include 20 issues per 30-day cycle; additional issues cost $4 each. 20 included, then $4.00 / issue 20 included, then $4.00 / issue 20 included, then $4.00 / issue Custom 
 Issue rejections The included allowance for rejecting surfaced issues during each 30-day billing cycle. 20 20 20 Custom 
 Logs ingested The volume of log data sent to Sazabi. Hobby, Startup, and Growth include 200 GB per cycle; additional data costs $0.10 per GB. 200 GB included, then $0.10/GB 200 GB included, then $0.10/GB 200 GB included, then $0.10/GB Custom 
 Logs scanned The volume of log data scanned when running queries, measured separately from ingestion. Includes 50 GB per cycle on Hobby, Startup, and Growth, then $0.10 per GB. 50 GB included, then $0.10/GB 50 GB included, then $0.10/GB 50 GB included, then $0.10/GB Custom 
 AI usage AI usage consumes credits. Extra credits cost $0.01 each. Hobby, Startup, and Growth receive 500 credits per cycle; unused credits carry forward. 500 credits included, then $0.01/credit 500 credits included, then $0.01/credit 500 credits included, then $0.01/credit Custom 
 Automation runs Executions of your configured automations. Hobby, Startup, and Growth include 5,000 runs per cycle; additional runs cost $0.01 each. 5,000 included, then $0.01 / run 5,000 included, then $0.01 / run 5,000 included, then $0.01 / run Custom

 What happens when I go over my included usage? 
 Hobby, Startup, and Growth each include 200 GB of logs, 50 GB of logs scanned, 20 issues, and 5,000 automation runs per cycle. Additional logs ingested and logs scanned cost $0.10 per GB, additional issues cost $4 each, and additional automation runs cost $0.01 each. Each plan also includes 20 issue rejections per cycle.
