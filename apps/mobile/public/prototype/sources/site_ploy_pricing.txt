 How credits work
 Credits are the fuel for the work Ploy does. They're spent while Ploy is actively building or editing — a workspace that's just live and serving traffic uses next to nothing.
 Professional websites cost more to build
 Ploy may consume more credits while building a polished, AI website builder for marketing teams . The work is real engineering and design — not a template fill — so heavier builds draw more.
 A slurp extracts your brand, then credits make it real
 A slurp isn't a 100% clone. It's a best-effort, deterministic (no-AI) pass that pulls your brand's soul — structure, identity, design tokens. Credits then turn that into professional-grade Astro and populate your design system — work that costs thousands of dollars with a development team .
 One balance, unified across everything
 Image generation, code generation, document generation, planning, and visitor enrichment all draw from the same credit balance. No separate meters to track.
 Ploybooks consume credits
 Running a Ploybook — one-off or on a recurring schedule — spends credits as it works through each step. AEO checks work the same way, with no prompt cap; see how that compares with HubSpot AEO .
 Built to get radically more efficient
 We're building Ploy to be 100× more time- and cost-efficient than the old way of shipping marketing sites — and we're constantly improving credit efficiency as the platform gets smarter.

 Pricing questions 
 Pricing, answered.
 Do I need a credit card to start?
 No. The Free tier needs no card and includes a generous daily credit allotment so you can build and ship real pages before you ever pay.
 How do credits work?
 Credits are spent while Ploy is actively building or editing your site. An idle workspace uses minimal credits. Free includes 2,000 credits; Starter and Pro include a monthly allotment, with higher credit tiers selectable on each paid plan.
 Can I change plans anytime?
 Yes. Monthly plans have no long-term commitment, so you can upgrade or downgrade whenever your needs change.
 Can I pay annually?
 Yes. Starter and Pro can be billed monthly or annually. Annual billing costs 20% less: Starter starts at $480 a year ($40 a month) and Pro at $2,880 a year ($240 a month).
 When do I need Pro instead of Starter?
 Starter unlocks custom domains, Google integrations, and GitHub Code Sync. Move to Pro when you want Ad integrations, HubSpot, or Attio , plus the larger monthly credit allotment for active teams.
 What does Enterprise add?
 Enterprise includes everything in Pro plus Salesforce, white-glove onboarding, done-for-you Ploybook setup, enterprise terms, SSO, and SOC 2 Type II. Custom Ploybooks can be built on any plan — Enterprise adds a team to build them for you. Pricing and credits are custom — talk to sales.
 Is my data used to train AI models?
 No. Ploy does not use customer data to train AI models on any plan, including Free, self-serve paid plans, and Enterprise.
 What counts as a custom domain?
 A family means the domain and its subdomains that share the same registrable root. For example, example.com, www.example.com, and shop.example.com are one family, so they use one slot even if they are attached to different sites. example.co.uk and shop.example.co.uk also form one family. Ploy uses the Public Suffix List to handle endings like .co.uk correctly.
